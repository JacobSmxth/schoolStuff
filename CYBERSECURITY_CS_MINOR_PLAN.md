# BS Cybersecurity with a Computer Science Minor

## Goal

Complete the **Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity with a minor in Computer
Science by Summer 2027** while:

- keeping Fall 2026 unchanged;
- avoiding an overload above 15 credit hours;
- preserving HOPE Scholarship eligibility;
- completing the prerequisite sequence for the Cybersecurity capstone; and
- retaining useful computer science coursework for cybersecurity employment.

This is a planning document, not an official degree audit. DegreeWorks, the
academic advisor, the Computer Science and Cybersecurity Department, and
Financial Aid have final authority over degree requirements, prerequisites,
course substitutions, scheduling, and financial-aid eligibility.

## Critical Prerequisite Chain

CYBR 4950 has a published prerequisite of **CSCI 3300**.

CSCI 3300 requires a grade of C or better in **CSCI 3200 or CSCI 3410**.
Because CSCI 3200 is scheduled for Fall 2026, the normal sequence is:

```text
Fall 2026: CSCI 3200
        ↓
Spring 2027: CSCI 3300
        ↓
Summer 2027: CYBR 4950
```

CSCI 3300 may not appear as a named Cybersecurity requirement in DegreeWorks,
but it is effectively required unless the department waives the CYBR 4950
prerequisite.

## Elective Priorities

Choose electives in this order:

1. A course explicitly required by the final DegreeWorks audit.
2. A course that is reliably offered in Summer 2027.
3. A course relevant to the potential job.
4. A course that preserves the required upper-division total.
5. A course with a manageable summer workload.

Potential employment-focused subjects include:

- cloud computing or cloud security;
- operating systems;
- systems administration;
- advanced Linux;
- secure software development;
- penetration testing;
- incident response;
- digital forensics;
- applied machine learning for security; or
- an approved cybersecurity internship.

Do not select the final electives solely by course title. Confirm in DegreeWorks
that each course fills the intended requirement before registering.

### What HOPE should cover

The recommended 15/15/10 schedule never exceeds HOPE's 15-hour award limit for
a semester. All 40 remaining hours should therefore be eligible for a HOPE
award, subject to continued eligibility.

However, "HOPE-funded" does not mean the total student bill is zero. For FY
2027:

- UNG bachelor tuition is approximately **$193 per credit hour**;
- the HOPE award is **$191 per credit hour**;
- the estimated tuition difference over 40 hours is approximately **$80**; and
- HOPE does not pay mandatory fees, books, housing, food, or most course-specific
  charges.

Using approximately $590 in mandatory fees for each full-load term:

| Remaining direct charge | Rough estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| Tuition difference after HOPE | $80 |
| Fall 2026 mandatory fees | $590 |
| Spring 2027 mandatory fees | $590 |
| Summer 2027 mandatory fees | $590 |
| **Estimated remaining tuition and mandatory fees** | **$1,850** |

Books, inclusive-access materials, course charges, and the graduation fee are
additional. Pell Grant, institutional scholarships, or other aid could reduce
the personal balance, potentially to zero.

## Could Graduation Move to Spring 2027?

Spring 2027 graduation is not part of the recommended plan.

It would require completing all 40 remaining hours across Fall 2026 and Spring
2027, approximately:

- Fall 2026: 21 hours
- Spring 2027: 19 hours

The largest academic barrier is the capstone sequence. Because CSCI 3200 is
being taken in Fall 2026, CSCI 3300 would normally be taken in Spring 2027, and
CYBR 4950 would follow it in Summer 2027.

Spring graduation would therefore require written permission to take CYBR 4950
concurrently with CSCI 3300, plus overload approval and a workable course
schedule. Even if approved, the workload and schedule-conflict risk would be
high, and HOPE would pay for no more than 15 hours in each semester.

Summer 2027 is the safer and more financially efficient graduation target.
